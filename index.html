ABOUT

I work on methods that make model adaptation easier to specify and reuse, especially in the age of large language models. My recent work explores how text and documents can directly produce LoRA adapters through hypernetwork-style systems.

Previously, my research focused on reinforcement learning, cooperative multi-agent learning, meta-learning, and distributed RL systems. I care about methods that are practically useful and clear enough to reproduce.
